On Saturday June 20th we held another Pi Jam with many or our regulars returning and a few new comers.

It was Philip's birthday and to mark the occasion he brought along a Raspberry Pie,
(well bakewell tart), which did not last long at all.

{{< img src="cake1.jpeg" title="Raspberry Pie "align="center" >}}

Steve brought along some of his Raspberry PI based robots, using the original model B, A+, PI0W and the Pico.

{{< img src="mec2.jpeg" title="Meccanum Robot "align="center" >}}

The meccanum robot is controllable from a website, and several of the youngsters had a go with it.
As is the case with these events, Steve had tested it from his PI500 at home but used an Iphone on the day.
What could go wrong, well quite a bit, some of the button presses did not work!
It might be fixed for the next PI Jam and use the camera for line following, but then again...

If you are interested it uses a Pimoroni motor2040 for motor control and an ESP32-CAM for the web control.
All based on one of Kevs robots at
www.kevsrobots.com, Steve's goto website for robot projects.
One day Steve will either take better pictures or learn how to edit them!

Tony,
